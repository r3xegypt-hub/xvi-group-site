# INSPIRATION BOARD — Reference Analysis

> Why premium sites feel premium. What we adopt. What we reject.

---

## 1. Slalom — `slalom.com`

### What We Observe
- **Light palette dominance.** White backgrounds, generous whitespace, soft shadows — no dark hero section
- **Warmth over tech.** People photography, collaboration imagery, approachable language
- **Subtle motion.** Elements fade in on scroll, no flashy hero animations, cursor-responsive but restrained
- **Card-based layout.** Clean floating cards with thin borders and slight shadow elevation
- **Typography hierarchy.** Large bold headlines with clear size contrast to body text
- **Navigation.** Mega menu with clean categorization, search prominent, language switcher visible
- **Footer depth.** Multi-column mega footer with brand colors, logo, and structured links

### What We Adopt
- Light luxury card system with subtle `box-shadow` and `border: 1px solid rgba(201, 169, 110, 0.12)`
- Generous whitespace rhythm — sections breathe, content never crowds
- Scroll-triggered fade-in animations (opacity 0 → 1, translateY 20px → 0)
- Warm photography tone — professional but not sterile
- Clear typographic hierarchy with mathematical scale

### What We Reject
- Generic stock photography feel — we need more editorial, curated imagery
- Overly corporate tone — we are premium but not boring
- No geometric visual language — Slalom lacks our XII motif
- Color palette too safe — our Executive Gold adds distinction they lack

## 2. Artefact — `artefact.com`

### What We Observe
- **Dark elegance.** Dark backgrounds with bright accent text — high contrast luxury
- **Motion-first.** Scroll-triggered animations, parallax, text reveal, SVG morphing
- **Strong typography.** Oversized serif/sans combinations, editorial headline treatment
- **Geometric patterns.** Abstract shapes, grids, and mathematical compositions
- **Creative portfolio energy.** Project showcases with image galleries, case study cards
- **Cursor interactions.** Custom cursor effects, hover state richness
- **Bilingual support.** EN/FR toggle integrated naturally into navigation

### What We Adopt
- Motion DNA — scroll-triggered reveals, text split animations, parallax depth
- Geometric pattern language — the XII diamond as architectural element
- Editorial typography — oversized headlines that command attention
- Case study showcase format — project cards with hover reveal
- Bilingual architecture — clean EN/AR toggle (we use Arabic, not French)

### What We Reject
- Full dark theme — our Light Luxury direction requires light backgrounds
- Overly artistic/abstract — we need executive credibility, not gallery energy
- Complex cursor effects on mobile — desktop only for hover interactions
- Too much motion — we need restraint (Apple level, not After Effects level)

---

## 3. McKinsey / QuantumBlack — `mckinsey.com`

### What We Observe
- **Authority design.** Clean, structured, data-forward — every element communicates expertise
- **Content depth.** Long-form articles, research papers, insight cards — information architecture is king
- **Restrained palette.** Blue + white + minimal accent — let content speak
- **Typography precision.** Consistent scale, clear hierarchy, readable at every breakpoint
- **Section rhythm.** Hero → stats → featured insights → CTAs → footer — predictable but effective
- **Trust signals.** Client logos, statistics, awards prominently displayed
- **Navigation depth.** Deep mega menus with categorization, industries, capabilities

### What We Adopt
- Content-first information architecture — every section serves a purpose
- Statistics and trust signals prominently featured (stats counter, client logos, awards)
- Clean section rhythm — Hero → Stats → Services → Insights → CTA → Footer
- Typography precision — consistent mathematical scale across all breakpoints
- Industry/capability matrix — show breadth and depth simultaneously

### What We Reject
- Too corporate/generic — we need more personality and warmth
- Overly text-heavy — we balance text with whitespace and visual elements
- Blue dominance — our Navy + Gold is more distinctive
- Static feel — our motion layer adds life without distraction
